What each part does

  • Base stat is fixed for the species and counts double.
  • IV (0 to 31) is the Pokémon's genes. At level 100 each point adds 1.
  • EVs come from battling. Every 4 EVs add 1 at level 100, up to 252 per stat and 510 in total.
  • Level scales the whole sum, so at level 50 IVs and EVs count half.
  • Nature multiplies the finished stat by 1.1 or 0.9. HP is never affected.
